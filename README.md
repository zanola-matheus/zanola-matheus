## What I'm building

### 💈 [Operazor](https://github.com/zanola-matheus/operazor) — [operazor.com](https://operazor.com)
Financial and operational management for barbershops and barbershop chains. Per-barber performance reporting, automated P&L and cash flow, budget vs. actual, and multi-unit consolidation. I lead engineering, taking it from a founder's internal prototype to a production SaaS. Live since June 2026.

### 📊 [GrowthRadar](https://github.com/zanola-matheus/growth-radar) — Shopify App Store
Analytics for Shopify merchants: revenue, orders, AOV, LTV, returns and customer mix in one place. Built on the MERN stack with Shopify Polaris. Live on the Shopify App Store since April 2026.

### 📉 Previously: [SubSignal](https://github.com/zanola-matheus/sub-signal)
A churn early-warning tool for SaaS founders: a weekly email digest, no dashboard. Shut down in 2026 after limited traction. Write-up in the repo.
